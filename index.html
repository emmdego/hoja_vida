02 · Approach

Learning philosophy

I approach every technology, system, or programming language from its foundations, documenting key ideas along the way. My goal is to build strong foundations, understand concepts instead of memorizing them, and reach the point where I can explain what I have learned to someone else in a clear and simple way.

I also document my practice scripts with comments and key ideas, so I can review what I learned and refresh my understanding if I ever forget something. Once the foundation is solid, I go deeper into more specific concepts and connect them with what I already know.

My learning path

I started with Python, which I believe was a good starting point. Alongside it I learned Object-Oriented Programming, SOLID principles, and Clean Architecture, which gave me a foundation in software design and architecture.

I now use these principles when learning new technologies, and I increasingly recognize similar ideas in other well-designed systems. That helps me understand new concepts faster and connect them with existing knowledge.

I document and comment my process using Git and save it to GitHub.
