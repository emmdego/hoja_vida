## 🧠 My Learning Philosophy

I have developed a learning and documentation philosophy that I follow whenever I study a new topic.

I approach every technology, system, or programming language from its foundations, documenting key ideas along the way. My goal is to build strong foundations, understand concepts instead of simply memorizing them, and reach a point where I can explain what I have learned to someone else in a clear and simple way.

I also document my practice scripts alongside comments and key ideas that can help me review what I learned and refresh my understanding if I ever forget something.

Once I have built a strong foundation, I can go deeper into more specific concepts and technologies while connecting them with what I already understand.


## 🛤️ My Learning Path

At the beginning of my studies, I believe I chose a good starting point: learning my first programming language, Python.

Alongside Python, I learned Object-Oriented Programming, SOLID principles, and Clean Architecture. This did not only help me understand Python, but also helped me build a solid mental foundation around software design and architecture.

Today, I use these principles when learning new technologies and systems, and I increasingly recognize similar ideas being applied in different technologies and well-designed systems. This helps me understand new concepts faster and connect them with knowledge I already have.

I always try to document and comment my process using Git and saving it to this GitHub.

Currently, I am focusing on backend development, SQL databases, and cybersecurity foundations. These areas are connected by my interest in good software design and architecture principles, particularly SOLID and the Single Responsibility Principle.
